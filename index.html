Adventure Guide

How to Play

  • Run & Jump: Use ← → (or A D) to run, and Space, ↑, or W to jump. On phones or tablets, use the big ◀ ▶ and ▲ JUMP buttons.
  • Golden ? Blocks: Bump them from below to pop out bonus coins, bouncy stars, and speed boosts!
  • No Stress Mode: You have infinite lives! If you fall into a gap or bump a sleepy critter, a friendly cloud places you right back on the nearest platform.
  • Unlock All 3 Scrolls: Grab the flagpole at the end of each world to read its mystery scroll and uncover the big secret at the Rainbow Castle!
