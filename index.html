Adventure Guide

How to Play

  • Run & Jump: Use ← → (or A D) to run, and Space, ↑, or W to jump. On phones or tablets, use the big ◀ ▶ and ▲ JUMP buttons.
  • Golden ? Blocks: Bump them from below to pop out bonus coins, bouncy stars, and mini-sparkles!
  • No Stress Mode: You have infinite lives! If you fall into a gap or bump a sleepy cloud critter, a friendly stork bubble places you right back on the nearest platform.
  • Collect All 3 Clues: Grab the flagpole at the end of Worlds 1-1 and 1-2 to read each rhyme clue, then reach the Castle in World 1-3 for the grand fireworks reveal!
